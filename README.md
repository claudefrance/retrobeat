# Retrobeat
Open Source Standalone Drum machine on a floppy disk 

.::Back to 1997 !::.

Retrobeat is a free standalone software drum machine for electronic music producers, initialy inspired by the classic PC program Hammerhead (Bram Bos - 1997). It lets you import your own samples, create, edit, play and export crunchy drumloops. The exported WAV files are perfectly looped and ready for your daw. You can also save your projet in the .rb format and edit it later ! And all of this fits on a single floppy disk!

.::Features::.

    8 independent tracks.
    2 Bars, 16/32 programmable old school steps sequencer.
    Parametrable shuffle and humanize function. 
    Individual track controls for volume, pitch, reverse, decay, filter, distortion, reverb and delay sync.
    Individual step volume and panoramic adjustment. 
    Built-in Compressor.
    Import mono or stereo WAV files (support Drag and drop).
    Export your loops in 16/24-bit stereo/mono 44.1/48Khz PCM WAV file.
    Save and open projects in the .rb format.
    Built-in mixer.
    Built-in browser with sample preview.
    Live View mode (experimental).

.: How to Install::.

The binaries are for Windows x64 only
This application is portable. Decompress Retrobeat-Windows-x64.zip where you want.

.: How to use::.

Space = Play/stop

Reverse sample = Right click on the sample wav and activate reverse

Individual step volume and panoramic adjustment = Right click on the step to access knobs.
